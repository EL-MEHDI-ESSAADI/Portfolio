# Portfolio direction, revised

Audience: engineering hiring teams evaluating frontend product ownership. Keep the approved content; change its visual presentation and remove location details.

## Tokens

Ink navy #203047, paper #F5F7FB, white #FFFFFF, cobalt #3866C7, muted slate #657187 and pale blue #E8EEF8. Space Grotesk carries display headings; DM Sans supplies more readable body and navigation text. Both fonts are local.

## Composition

```
Sticky white header: name / simple navigation / résumé
Wide typographic hero + approved portrait in an offset frame
Quiet horizontal practice strip
Large FIFA screen preview, then title + description
Four professional product cards with restrained category icons
Experience rows with clearly separated dates, employer and role
Grouped capability panels with low contrast skill chips
Readable about section and grounded AI practice
Navy contact panel
```

The signature is the human portrait balanced against oversized frontend positioning, followed by a large, readable view of the real FIFA booking interface. This creates a personal portfolio rather than a dense catalogue of small bordered boxes.

## Critique and choices

The previous version had small utility text, too many equal weight borders, a dark first screen and cramped project previews. Increase body type, simplify the hierarchy, open up spacing and give the real product interface more room. Avoid fabricated product screenshots, metric counters, numbered decorations and elaborate background effects. The portrait frame is the deliberate expressive element; keep the remaining layout calm.

## Résumé

Restore the original grey header, tracked uppercase headings, left contact/skills/education/language column, right profile/experience column and divider lines. Preserve the original Raleway and Lato font families. Use current content, omitting personal location. Condense experience wording where needed rather than shrinking the text excessively or changing the layout.
